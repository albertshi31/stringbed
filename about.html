About

A bit about me and why I made this.

Me

I've been stringing since I was 14 or 15. I played a ton of junior tennis growing up and broke strings pretty often, so learning how to string my own rackets made sense. It saved me a lot of money too.

In college I strung for my club team, and I got a lot of practice stringing rackets from that.

Why I made this

I feel like out of all of my friends, I'm one of the few people who actually likes stringing. I think it is really chill, and it takes my mind off things. Additionally, I couldn't find a good resource or website that teaches people how to string rackets, so I thought to build my own.

I wanted to make something that walks you through the whole process for stringing your racket, and clarify as many of the confusing details as possible.

What I'd really love to add someday is a 3D view of the whole process, where you can just pan around the racket and watch it get strung.

My setup

Machine
Gamma Progression II. It's a crank, and I've never really had any issues with it.
String
Solinco Tour Bite 16L. I was using Hyper-G until I was 18, but I switched because I felt like the launch angle was too high and the string became too unpredictable in my frame.

Strings

I've pretty much used Solinco my whole life. If you're thinking about using polyester, they're a great place to start, and they hold tension pretty well. Ones I'd try:

  • Hyper-G
  • Mach-10
  • Confidential
  • Confidential Soft
  • Tour Bite

I'm not sponsored, and not affiliated with them. I just like their products.

Stuff I've learned

  1. Don't overtighten your clamps, and grip the string with the clamp's teeth. I used to break clamps all the time and had no idea why. I fixed how I was clamping, and I haven't broken one in 5 years.
  2. You get faster by stringing more. My first few rackets took 1.5 to 3 hours. Some would break while I would be stringing crosses and that felt miserable. However, now it takes me around 15 to 20 minutes to string a racket, and I feel like you can achieve that speed in a couple of years. Once you feel like you're in a good place, you can start a stringing business as a side hustle!
