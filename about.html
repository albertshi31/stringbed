About

A bit about me and why I made this.

Me

I've been stringing since I was 14 or 15. I played a ton of junior tennis growing up and broke strings constantly, so learning to do it myself just made sense. It saved me a lot of money too.

In college I strung for my club team, which is where I got most of my reps in.

Why I made this

I'm probably one of the few people who actually likes stringing. It's chill and it takes my mind off things.

I wanted to make something that walks you through the whole thing for your racket, so it's less intimidating the first time.

What I'd really love to add someday is a 3D view of the whole process, where you can just pan around the racket and watch it get strung.

My setup

Machine
Gamma Progression II. It's a crank and honestly I've never had an issue with it.
String
Solinco Tour Bite. I was on Hyper-G until I was 18, then switched.

Strings

I've used Solinco my whole life. If you're getting into poly, they're a great place to start, and they hold tension pretty well. Ones I'd try:

  • Hyper-G
  • Mach-10
  • Confidential
  • Confidential Soft
  • Tour Bite

Not sponsored, not affiliated with anyone. I just like them.

Stuff I've learned

  1. Don't overtighten your clamps, and grip the string with the teeth. I used to break clamps all the time and had no idea why. Once I fixed how I was clamping, I haven't broken one in 5 years.
  2. You get faster by stringing more. That's really it. My first few took 1.5 to 3 hours. Now it's 15 to 20 minutes, and once you're quick you can make some money stringing for friends and teammates.
